      What happens after the crew sends it

      1. 1An incident number is createdAutomatic and in sequence, in NLEX's format, for example N0000124.
      2. 2Time and person are stampedDate and military time, the crew member's ID, and whether the km post came from GPS or was typed.
      3. 3TCR sees it at onceIt lands on the live board and on the km-post strip for that section.
      4. 4Nothing is forgottenIf it is still open after 2 hours, TCR gets an alarm.
      5. 5No signal is not a problemThe report is saved on the phone and sends by itself when the signal is back, with the time it was logged. Our answer to "poor connectivity", a patrol concern in NLEX's requirements.

TCR Personnel create the ones spotted on CCTV or reported through the hotline, dispatch resources in the field, and update and complete the event form. TCR Specialists finalize all incident types except road crashes and major incidents; TCR Supervisors finalize all types.
