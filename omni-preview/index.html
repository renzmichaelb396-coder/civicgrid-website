Why the crew list matters

What happens after the crew sends it

  1. 1An incident number is createdAutomatic and in sequence, for example OMNI-2026-000124.
  2. 2Time and person are stampedDate and military time, plus the crew member's ID.
  3. 3TCR sees it at onceIt lands on the live board and on the km-post strip for that section.
  4. 4Nothing is forgottenIf it is still open after 2 hours, TCR gets an alarm.
