The Problem

When disaster strikes, coordination breaks down before response does.

When disaster strikes, a Philippine LGU has to coordinate up to 15 response clusters — police, health, engineering, social welfare, logistics — over radios, group chats, and paper logs. There's no shared incident record. No dispatch audit trail. No way to show COA, the Mayor, or the public afterward that the response actually happened the way it's being described.

Data Privacy

How We Handle Your Data

In line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), here's what we collect on this site and through the Alerto PWA, and why.

What We Collect

  • Alerto PWA (citizen SOS reporting): your approximate location (geolocation), a description of the incident you're reporting, and an optional photo. No account or login is required to submit a report.
  • Contact form on this site: your name, agency or organization, email address, and anything you write in the optional message field.

Why We Collect It

Alerto PWA data is used to route your report to the appropriate CDRRMD response cluster and to maintain an auditable incident record for the LGU you're reporting to. Contact form data is used only to respond to your inquiry about CivicGrid's products.

Retention

Incident reports are retained as part of the LGU's official incident record, consistent with standard government recordkeeping practice. Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry.

Your Rights & Contact

Under RA 10173, you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data. For privacy concerns or requests, contact us at contact@civicgrid.dev.
