Will this add work for my staff?

I learn your forms, clusters and steps first, then build around them. Residents install nothing: it’s a link on any phone. Your team gets updates by text as an incident moves, and the command dashboard is made for your large EOC screens.

What if something goes wrong during a disaster?

The system runs on cloud servers, not on my computer, so your team keeps working even when I’m offline. During an emergency, messages from your office come first. What support covers is written in your contract.

Does it work without internet?

No. It needs an internet connection, so keep your hotline and radio in place.

How your data is handled

What is collected on this site and through the citizen report page, why, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What is collected

  • Citizen report page: your approximate location, a description of the incident, and an optional photo. No account or login is needed.
  • This site’s form: your name, office, email, any number you choose to give, and anything you write in the message.

Why

Citizen reports are routed to the right response cluster and kept as an auditable incident record for the LGU you’re reporting to. Form data is used only to reply to your inquiry, and reaches us through the form service Formspree.

Retention

Incident reports are kept as part of the LGU’s official incident record, in line with government recordkeeping practice. Form messages are kept only as long as needed to reply, in Formspree and in email.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal data, and to delete what you sent through this form. Incident reports follow the LGU’s records rules, so the LGU decides those requests. Write to Renz Michael Bautista at contact@civicgrid.dev.
